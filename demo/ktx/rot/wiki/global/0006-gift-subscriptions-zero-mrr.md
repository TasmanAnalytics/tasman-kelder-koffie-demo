# 0006: Gift subscriptions carry zero MRR and sit outside the churn base

Date: 2025-11-18 · Status: accepted · Owner: Sanne (data), agreed with Femke (finance)

Gift subscriptions are prepaid for three shipments and then expire. The purchase is revenue once, at
checkout. Their MRR is zero by design, and they are excluded from the churn base and from churn.
